Inquiries

Frequently asked questions.

Clear, unvarnished policies on installation, age restrictions, and streak resets.

Why is WillWar strictly 18+ only? expand_more
WillWar is built for mature adults navigating digital sobriety and sexual self-regulation. We maintain an uncompromising boundary to guarantee that all microcopy, evidence discussions, and features remain frank, clinical, and intended solely for independent adults.
How does it install from Safari without an App Store? expand_more
WillWar is an engineered Progressive Web App (PWA). In Safari on your iPhone, tap the Share icon and select "Add to Home Screen". It runs in its own full-screen container with offline capability, swift performance, and zero App Store surveillance.
What happens if I slip and reset my streak? expand_more
There is zero shame or gamified penalty. The app asks for a simple, optional trigger tag (e.g., "Late night exhaustion") to populate your insights. Your pact members see your number return to Day 0, but never the cause. Your historical days logged remain permanently in your journal.
Can anyone in my pact see my private journal notes? expand_more
Never. Pacts only broadcast two data points: your anonymized handle and your current streak duration. Journals, urge timestamps, and personal reflections are isolated inside your device storage.
Does the clock work offline on airplanes or without data? expand_more
Yes. The chronometer derives its state from hardware epoch time. You can play Drift and Swell completely offline. Pact synchronization occurs silently the moment your connection re-establishes.
Is WillWar considered medical therapy? expand_more
No. WillWar is a self-directed behavioral accountability tool, not psychotherapy, medical treatment, or clinical intervention. If you are struggling with compulsive behavioral disorders that impair daily functioning, please consult a licensed mental health professional.
How does the War Chest handle payments and charity transfers? expand_more
Collateral is held in segregated trust. If a reset occurs, that fractional sum is sent directly via payment partner to the verified 501(c)(3) or charitable trust you selected. Upon successful completion of your 30-day challenge on December 1st, 100% of remaining funds return to your originating payment method. WillWar charges zero brokerage fees on pledges.
