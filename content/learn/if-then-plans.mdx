## What an if-then plan is

A goal says what you want. An if-then plan says what you will do when a specific moment arrives:

- If it's after 10 pm and I'm on my phone in bed, then I put it on the charger in the hall.
- If I'm bored and alone on a Saturday night, then I go for a walk or call someone.
- If I feel an urge building, then I tap Go a round before I decide anything.

Psychologists call these implementation intentions.

## Why it works

When you link a cue to an action in advance, the decision is already made. In the moment, you don't have to argue with yourself; you follow the plan. Research suggests the cue becomes easier to notice and the action starts more automatically.

## What the evidence says

A 2006 meta-analysis pooled 94 studies across health, study and work goals. People who made if-then plans reached their goals noticeably more often than people who only set the goal. That is unusually consistent evidence for a simple tool.

## Do it now

Write three plans for your riskiest moments. Be specific about the when and where, and make the action small enough to do even on a bad night. Look at your reset log if you have one; it shows where the plans are needed most.
