## What people notice

Ask anyone doing a challenge like this when it gets hard and many will say the same thing: late at night, alone, phone in hand. Your own reset tags may say it too.

## What the research says, and doesn't

Some findings are fairly solid. Adults who use their phones in bed tend to go to sleep later and sleep less. That much shows up across several studies, though most of them can't prove cause and effect.

Other ideas are shakier. A popular theory held that self-control works like a fuel tank that drains through the day, which would explain why evenings are hard. Large, careful replication attempts have not found the effect. So the "you've run out of willpower" story is not well supported.

What's left is a simpler explanation: late nights combine tiredness, boredom, privacy and an endless feed. That's a lot of cues in one place.

## Design the room, not the willpower

You don't need to win the argument at midnight if you set things up at 9 pm:

- Phone out of the bedroom. Charge it in another room and use a cheap alarm clock.
- A set lights-out time. Pick one and set a reminder 30 minutes before.
- Night watch. WillWar can stay quiet until morning, or check in late if you turn it on.

## The honest grade

Mixed. The environment changes are low-cost and sensible. The science behind why evenings are hard is still being worked out.
